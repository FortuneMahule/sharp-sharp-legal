Sharp Sharp! Privacy Policy

Plain English. What we collect, why, and how to see it or delete it.

Last updated: 6 October 2026. This policy follows the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa.

The short version. We keep a name you choose, your province, and your game results, so the Daily 10, duels and the province league work. We show ads (Google AdMob) and sell an optional upgrade (Sharp Sharp Plus). We do not sell your information. You can delete everything yourself, inside the app, in under a minute: Me > Delete my account.

3. Who else handles your information

We use these companies to run the game. They may only use your information to provide their service to us (and, for Google ads, as described in section 2).

  • Supabase (database and login service). Our database runs in London, United Kingdom.
  • Google: AdMob (ads), Sign-In (optional), Google Play (the store and payments), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivering notifications).
  • RevenueCat (keeps track of Sharp Sharp Plus purchases).
  • Expo (passes notifications on to Google's delivery service).

Some of these companies are based outside South Africa, for example in the United Kingdom and the United States. By using the app you agree that your information can be sent to them for this purpose. POPIA allows this where the receiving country or company has laws or agreements that protect your information in a similar way, and we choose providers that do.

Other players see only your display name and province (for example in a duel or the result of one). They never see your account number, your answers to other games, or anything from the table above that is not part of that duel.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else, unless the law forces us to.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your game information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, everything linked to you is removed from our database straight away (see section 6). Anonymous usage counts have nothing to link back to you, so they stay. Backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten as part of their normal cycle.

5. Your rights under POPIA

You may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask for a copy;
  • ask us to correct it (you can change your name and province yourself in Me > Name and province);
  • ask us to delete it (you can do this yourself, see below);
  • object to us using it, or withdraw a permission you gave (for example switch off notifications in Me, or change your ad choice in Me > Ad privacy settings);
  • complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your information has been handled wrongly: Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191; enquiries@inforegulator.org.za; inforegulator.org.za. If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).

To make a request, write to fortuneshanemahule@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days, free of charge. We may need to confirm it is really you before we act.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Me, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete my account, then confirm.

This removes, from our database, your name, province, account number, streak, league points, Daily 10 results, duels and their answers, notification address and Plus status, and it deletes your login (including a linked Google sign-in). It cannot be undone. The app on your phone starts again from the beginning.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Plus subscription. Cancel that in the Google Play app (Payments & subscriptions) first. Ads data that Google already holds under Google's own privacy policy is managed in your Google account.

No phone? Email fortuneshanemahule@gmail.com with your display name and province, and we will delete it for you.

7. Keeping your information safe

  • Our database allows each player to see only their own results. The right answers to questions are kept on our server and are never sent to your phone before you answer.
  • Information travels between the app and our server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Our secret keys are never put inside the app.

No system is perfectly safe. If something goes wrong that puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

8. Children

Sharp Sharp! is a general-audience quiz game. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 and the app is not aimed at them. In South Africa, people under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission to have their personal information handled, so if you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you play. A parent can ask us to delete a child's account at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or why, we will update this page and the date at the top, and tell players in the app before the change starts to apply.
